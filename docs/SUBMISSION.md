# Submission and judging

Source: kickoff slides (Nov 1, 2026). The checklist and the schedule are in PLAN.md 13 and 18. This file adds the judging
details, so we build toward the score.

## Judging: 8 items, each scored 0–3 (24 total)
1. **Use of Google tech**
   a. Google tech is the core of the product and works effectively.
   b. The reasons for choosing it are rational and concrete → PLAN.md 16 ("Rationale / Pros and Cons").
2. **Product completeness**
   a. The steps needed to solve the problem work as one connected flow → the demo script (PLAN.md 17) must run end to end.
   b. Target users can reach their goal through the UI/UX.
   c. The design considers security, scalability and maintainability.
3. **Solving a social problem**
   a. The problem is concrete and clear, with evidence (PLAN.md 1 + survey).
   b. The features actually help solve it.
4. **Pitch**
   a. The demo and story are clear and have impact. Demo-first: what we built and why → demo.

Not scored but required: where and how AI was used and why (`docs/ai-usage.md`), and what we learned.

## Disqualifiers
Faked demo video · third-party AI tools front and center · off-theme or off-category (must be **Tourism**) ·
inappropriate content · rule violations · missing required items · not running on a Google Cloud runtime.

## Submission mechanics
- Google Form per venue: the GitHub URL + a Google Drive folder set to "Anyone with the link can view".
- The Drive folder holds the product document (official template), the demo video, the attribution sheet, and the pitch slides (in-person only).
- After 19:00 on Nov 10 the repo is read-only until Regional Day ends. The deployed URL must work until Nov 14.
